11-hour layover in NYC. Landed at JFK at 5am, jumped on the A train, and caught sunrise on the Brooklyn Bridge. Spent the day bouncing between Brooklyn and Manhattan rooftops with friends—Chrysler Building, Empire State, Hudson Yards—before racing back to JFK for my connection to Paris.

Layovers don't have to be boring.
