This is some of the most demanding flying I've ever done—tight spacing, constant focus, and precision coordination with a group of skilled pilots all preparing for the legendary arrival into Oshkosh.

B2Osh is more than just a flight to AirVenture. It's a team effort built on trust, training, and a love for aviation. Our formation training in the Pacific Northwest covered station keeping, welded wing turns, and trail transitions, with coaches pushing us to fly tighter, safer, and smoother.

Special thanks to our formation coaches and to Hornet Hunter Photography for the amazing coverage of the event.
