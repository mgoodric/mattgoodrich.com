Prague after dark. These cobblestone streets and centuries-old architecture hit different at night—every corner felt like stepping into another era. The kind of place that makes you slow down and just look up.

It was a work trip, so I was only able to get out with my camera at night. But honestly, that might have been the best way to see this city.
